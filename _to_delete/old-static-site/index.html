Who we are

Nature's Wealth, Our Priority

Metallic Ore Limited is a licensed miner, dealer and exporter of gold and diamonds based at Wilkinson Road, Freetown. We connect Sierra Leone's natural resources with buyers around the world through transparent, professional and responsible trade.

From alluvial and rock gold to rough diamonds and industrial minerals, we handle sourcing, assaying, documentation and secure export so our partners can buy with confidence.
